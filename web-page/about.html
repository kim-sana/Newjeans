How it all started

Four chapters of a group that arrived quietly and changed the conversation around K-pop.

One of a kind
The beginning

Debut Days

NewJeans debuted in July 2022 under ADOR, a HYBE subsidiary. With zero individual member reveals prior to launch, their first single 'Attention' exploded globally within hours — a mystery debut that rewrote K-pop marketing.

Growing together

Global Recognition

NewJeans achieved a Guinness World Record as the fastest K-pop act to reach 1 billion Spotify streams. They topped the Billboard 200 with 'Get Up' and became the first K-pop girl group to grace the stage at Lollapalooza.

Growing together

Bunnies Community

Their fandom, Bunnies (Tokki), grew rapidly as the group released hit after hit. From 'Hype Boy' to 'Ditto', each release became a cultural moment shared by millions worldwide.

A sound that stays close

Musical Identity

Their sound blends 90s/2000s R&B and pop with modern K-pop production. Known for their 'girl next door' image, the group creates music that feels intimate, honest, and instantly familiar.

Group history

A timeline of moments that shaped NewJeans

From a mystery debut to global stages — every milestone that made them who they are.

July 2022

Mystery Debut

Debuted with 'Attention' — no teasers, no member reveals. The song exploded globally within hours of release.

August 2022

1st EP 'New Jeans'

Debut EP featuring 'Attention', 'Hype Boy', 'Cookie', and 'Hurt'. 'Hype Boy' became the longest-running song on Billboard Global 200 by a K-pop female act.

January 2023

'OMG' Single Album

'Ditto' became the longest-running #1 on Circle Digital Chart and their first Billboard Hot 100 entry.

July 2023

'Get Up' Mini Album

'Super Shy', 'ETA', 'Cool With You'. Topped Billboard 200. First K-pop girl group to perform at Lollapalooza Chicago.
